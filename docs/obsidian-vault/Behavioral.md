# Behavioral Design Map

## Source Document
Full details: [[../behavioral-engagement-system.md]]

## Three Core Barriers
1. **Trust** - "Can I trust food from a stranger?"
2. **Social** - "Will my neighbors judge me for selling food?"
3. **Effort** - "Is this app worth learning?"

## Onboarding Nudges
- 3 slides, each addressing one barrier
- Coach marks: one per screen, one-tap dismiss, never repeated
- Progressive disclosure: features revealed by action, not time

## Engagement Loops
- **Daily hook**: "Today's Menu" — natural food temporality
- **Notifications**: 5 tiers, max 3 non-transactional/day
- **Badges** (subtle):
  - Seller: Trusted Cook, Quick Responder, Neighborhood Favorite
  - Buyer: Food Explorer, Community Supporter

## Trust Building
- Thumbs up/down (not stars) — private negative feedback
- "X neighbors ordered from this cook"
- Activity indicators: "Active this week", "Quick responder"
- New seller bootstrapping strategy for cold-start

## Retention
- Seller: income visibility, recognition, social connection
- Buyer: variety, discovery, convenience
- Re-engagement: hard stop after 60 days dormancy

## Anti-Patterns (NEVER)
- No confirmshaming
- No artificial urgency
- No fake scarcity
- No inflated metrics
- No dark patterns
